Title: Embedded Software Engineer - Edge AI
Company: SenseEdge Robotics

SenseEdge Robotics builds smart cameras for warehouse robots. We are hiring an Embedded Software Engineer to bring perception models onto resource-constrained hardware.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of embedded software development in C and C++
- Experience with embedded systems running embedded Linux on ARM processors
- Python for tooling, test automation, and model conversion scripts
- Working knowledge of computer vision pipelines (camera capture, preprocessing, inference post-processing)
- Comfortable profiling memory, CPU, and power on target hardware

Responsibilities:
- Port and optimize vision inference pipelines to ARM-based edge devices
- Integrate camera drivers and sensor interfaces with the perception stack
- Write and maintain board support code and system services
- Collaborate with the ML team to meet latency and accuracy targets on device
- Build hardware-in-the-loop tests for releases

Nice to have:
- Experience with TensorFlow Lite, ONNX Runtime, or TensorRT
- OpenCV and PyTorch experience
- RTOS experience for co-processor firmware
- Docker-based cross-compilation and CI/CD pipelines
