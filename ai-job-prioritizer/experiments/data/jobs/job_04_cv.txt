Title: Computer Vision Engineer
Company: VisionAI Technologies

VisionAI Technologies is seeking a Computer Vision Engineer to develop next-generation vision systems for autonomous systems and industrial applications.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in computer vision research or engineering
- Expert knowledge of CNN architectures, object detection (YOLO, Faster R-CNN, SSD), and segmentation
- Strong hands-on experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Proficiency with OpenCV, image processing, and computer vision algorithms
- Experience deploying vision models on edge devices (NVIDIA Jetson, mobile, embedded)
- Understanding of 3D vision: SLAM, depth estimation, point clouds (PCL, Open3D)
- Strong C++ and Python skills

Responsibilities:
- Develop and optimize computer vision algorithms for real-world applications
- Train, fine-tune, and deploy vision models for production environments
- Optimize models for performance, memory, and latency constraints
- Collaborate with robotics and embedded teams for system integration
- Evaluate model performance on diverse datasets and real-world scenarios

Nice to have:
- Experience with diffusion models, generative vision, or video analysis
- Contributions to open-source CV projects (Detectron2, MMDetection)
- Experience with simulation environments (AirSim, Gazebo)
