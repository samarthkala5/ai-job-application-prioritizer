Job ID: job_01_backend
Title: Backend Software Engineer
Company: CloudScale Inc.

We are looking for a Backend Software Engineer to join our Platform team and build the infrastructure that powers our core product.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional experience in backend development using Python or Go
- Strong knowledge of RESTful API design, HTTP, and gRPC protocols
- Experience with PostgreSQL, Redis, and message brokers such as RabbitMQ or Apache Kafka
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS preferred)
- Experience with microservices architecture, event-driven systems, and distributed systems
- Strong understanding of system design, performance optimization, and observability

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement backend services for high-traffic production systems
- Collaborate with frontend, mobile, and data teams to define and deliver APIs
- Optimize database queries, caching strategies, and service latency
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and internal developer tooling
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to engineering culture

Nice to have:
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect or similar
- Experience with Terraform or IaC tools
- OpenTelemetry and distributed tracing experience
- Kubernetes certification
