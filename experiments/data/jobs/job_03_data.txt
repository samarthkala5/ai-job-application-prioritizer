Job ID: job_03_data
Title: Data Analyst
Company: Analytics Corp

Analytics Corp is hiring a Data Analyst to transform data into actionable insights for business stakeholders.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Expert-level SQL for complex querying and data manipulation
- Proficiency in Python for data analysis (Pandas, NumPy)
- Experience with BI tools: Tableau, Power BI, or Looker
- Strong data visualization skills and storytelling ability
- Experience working with cross-functional teams (marketing, finance, operations)
- Ability to clean, validate, and prepare data from multiple sources

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain interactive dashboards and reports for executive leadership
- Perform ad-hoc analysis to answer business questions and identify trends
- Develop and maintain ETL pipelines for data ingestion and transformation
- Define and track KPIs, metrics, and business performance indicators
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders
- Collaborate with data engineers on data modeling and warehouse design

Nice to have:
- Experience with data warehousing (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift)
- A/B testing and experimental design knowledge
- Basic statistical knowledge (hypothesis testing, regression)
- Google Data Studio or Metabase experience
