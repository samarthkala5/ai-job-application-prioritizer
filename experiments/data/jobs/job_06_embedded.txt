Job ID: job_06_embedded
Title: Embedded Systems Engineer
Company: IoTech Labs

IoTech Labs is seeking an Embedded Systems Engineer to build firmware for our next-generation IoT devices and sensor systems.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in embedded firmware development
- Expert-level C and C++ programming for microcontrollers
- Strong experience with RTOS (FreeRTOS, Zephyr) and real-time systems
- Proficiency with ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers (STM32, Nordic nRF) and embedded Linux
- Knowledge of communication protocols: SPI, I2C, UART, CAN, BLE, LoRaWAN
- Experience with embedded development tools: GCC, GDB, JTAG, oscilloscopes
- Understanding of low-power design and power optimization techniques

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement embedded firmware for battery-powered IoT devices
- Read and integrate sensor datasheets and develop drivers for peripherals
- Optimize memory usage, CPU utilization, and power consumption
- Implement secure boot, OTA updates, and communication protocols
- Debug complex hardware-software integration issues using lab equipment
- Write and maintain embedded CI/CD pipelines and unit testing

Nice to have:
- Experience with wireless protocols (Bluetooth mesh, Zigbee, Thread)
- Automotive or industrial safety standards (ISO 26262, IEC 61508)
- Signal processing, sensor fusion, Kalman filters
- PCB design and electronics
