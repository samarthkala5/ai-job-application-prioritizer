Title: Machine Learning Engineer
Company: DeepTech AI

DeepTech AI is seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to productionize cutting-edge research and build scalable ML systems.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of industry experience in ML engineering or applied data science
- Expert-level Python with deep expertise in PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Strong background in NLP (transformers, BERT, GPT, T5) or Computer Vision (CNNs, YOLO, Vision Transformers)
- Experience with MLOps: MLflow, Docker, Kubernetes, model serving (Seldon, TFServing, Vertex AI)
- Data engineering: Pandas, PySpark, Airflow, feature stores
- Experience deploying models to production with monitoring, A/B testing, and rollback capabilities
- Understanding of model optimization: quantization, pruning, distillation, ONNX

Responsibilities:
- Build end-to-end ML pipelines from data ingestion to model deployment
- Collaborate with research teams to translate papers into production systems
- Design and implement model evaluation, monitoring, and drift detection
- Optimize inference latency and throughput for production workloads
- Contribute to internal ML platform and tooling

Nice to have:
- Publications in top ML conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR)
- Experience with LLM fine-tuning, RAG, or agent frameworks
- Cloud ML certifications (AWS ML Specialty, GCP ML Engineer)
