Job ID: job_09_ai_engineer
Title: AI Engineer (LLM Applications)
Company: Lumen Assist

Lumen Assist is looking for an AI Engineer to design, build, and operate LLM-powered product features such as document question answering and workflow assistants.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience building production software in Python
- Hands-on experience building applications on large language models (LLMs) via provider APIs or open-weight models
- Experience designing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines: chunking, embeddings, vector search, and reranking
- Building and deploying backend services with FastAPI
- Containerizing services with Docker and working with Git
- Ability to design evaluation sets and measure LLM output quality, latency, and cost

Responsibilities:
- Build and iterate on LLM features from prototype to production
- Own retrieval quality, prompt design, and guardrails for hallucination and safety
- Develop offline and online evaluation pipelines for model outputs
- Monitor production usage, cost, and latency, and optimize where needed
- Work with product managers to turn user problems into AI features

Nice to have:
- Experience fine-tuning models with PyTorch or Hugging Face
- Background in NLP or information retrieval
- Experience with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or agent frameworks
- Kubernetes and AWS deployment experience
